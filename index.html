Technical Specifications

We categorize our production into two main grades, engineered specifically for their distinct use cases.

Shisha / Hookah Grade

Premium 100%

Smokeless, odorless, and sparkless. Engineered for a clean taste, ensuring the pure flavor of your shisha tobacco.

Fixed Carbon > 80%
Calorific Value > 7300 kcal/kg
Ash Content < 2.5%
Ash Color White / Silver
Moisture < 5%
Volatile Matter < 15%
Burning Time 2 - 2.5 Hours
Available Shapes
Cube (22/25/26mm) Flat Hexagonal / Finger

BBQ Grade

High Heat

Formulated for intense, long-lasting heat suitable for grilling, roasting, and professional kitchen applications.

Fixed Carbon 75% - 80%
Calorific Value > 7000 kcal/kg
Ash Content 4% - 6%
Moisture < 6%
Burning Time 3 - 4 Hours
Available Shapes
Hexagonal (50mm x 100-200mm) Pillow / Oval

Packaging & Shipping

Presentation matters. We offer fully customizable packaging solutions to ensure your brand stands out on the shelf, while remaining secure during international transit.

Available in 1kg, 2kg, or 3kg options. Full color offset printing with optional UV coating, embossing, or hot stamping.

Master Carton

Durable corrugated boxes (10kg, 20kg) to protect inner boxes during shipping. Single color or full-color print available.

Minimum Order & Shipping

Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 1x20ft Container (approx. 18-19 Metric Tons). We ship globally under FOB or CIF terms from Tanjung Priok Port, Jakarta.
